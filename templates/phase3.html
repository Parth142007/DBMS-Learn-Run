Phase 3 — SQL & Relational Queries

Interactive mastery of DDL, DML, DQL, the 5 Relational JOINs (Inner, Left, Right, Self, Cross), Aggregate Functions, and Nested Subqueries.

Main Dashboard Phase 2: Intro to DBMS Schema Inspector
Phase 3 Learning Objectives

By completing this interactive laboratory, students will practice and master:

DDL Structures: Create, alter, drop, and truncate tables with integrity constraints.
DML Operations: Single & multi-row inserts, selective updates, safe deletes, and transaction behavior.
DQL Retrieval: Projections, aliases, 3-valued boolean logic, ORDER BY, and LIMIT.
5 Relational JOINs: INNER, LEFT, RIGHT, SELF, and CROSS joins with Cartesian risk analysis.
Aggregation & GROUP BY: COUNT, SUM, AVG, MIN, MAX, and HAVING vs WHERE execution order.
Nested Subqueries: Scalar, multi-row (IN/ANY/ALL), derived tables, and correlated EXISTS/NOT EXISTS.
