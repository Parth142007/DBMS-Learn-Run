Phase 6 — ER & Relational Model

Master Entity-Relationship Modeling, Attributes & Keys, Cardinality & Participation Constraints, ER-to-Relational Mapping, and Procedural Relational Algebra Operations.

Main Dashboard Phase 5: Normalization Schema Inspector
Module Learning Objectives

By completing this comprehensive interactive module on Entity-Relationship and Relational Modeling, students will be able to:

Construct Entity-Relationship Diagrams using standard Chen and Crow's Foot notations.
Classify attributes (composite, multivalued, derived) and identify Candidate, Primary, & Foreign Keys.
Distinguish Cardinality Ratios (1:1, 1:N, M:N) from Participation Constraints (Total vs Partial).
Model Weak Entities with partial keys and identifying relationships.
Apply Extended ER (EER) concepts: Specialization, Generalization, Disjoint/Overlapping constraints, and Aggregation.
Systematically Map ER Diagrams to Relational Schemas adhering to foreign-key and junction table rules.
Explain Relational Model Fundamentals: Tuples, Domains, Degree, Entity & Referential Integrity.
Execute and analyze Relational Algebra Operators ($\sigma, \pi, \cup, -, \times, \rho, \bowtie, \div$) in the Interactive Lab.
